## 1. Conversation Scan

1. Load the canonical [Growthub Conversation Scan](https://github.com/Growthub-OS/gh-client-agent-skills-library/blob/main/.agents/skills/creative-development/growthub-conversation-scan/SKILL.md) and its required references. Resolve products, audience, claims, competitors, previous angles and the current plan from source documents before searching.
2. Set a rolling **7-day** window. Extend to **10 days** only when needed for coverage and report the exact bounds, timezone and reason. For a delta against a prior scan, state the prior cutoff and actual coverage. Preserve the cutoff for any source with unresolved coverage.
3. Attempt the [required source families](PROCESS.md#required-source-coverage), including Twillory and competitor Facebook and Instagram ad comments, Reddit, first- and third-party reviews, and relevant video, social and forum discussions. Follow the canonical broad scan and drill-down. Open full source material and verify the individual comment or review date. For a fresh reply in an older thread, cite and date the reply itself.
4. Log each source family’s coverage and access gaps. Give each qualified finding an evidence ID, permalink, publication and capture dates, product, audience evidence, theme and objection. Preserve accurate short quotes or label paraphrases. Deduplicate and exclude brand replies, stale or undated items, and snippet-only leads from fresh findings. Mark recurring themes and explain whether they reprioritize work already in the queue.
5. Hand off the dated evidence ledger, source coverage, customer language, prioritized opportunities and unresolved gaps. Report the actual qualified count when evidence is thin. Each finding used in the plan must point to verified in-window evidence.

## 2. Ad Account Performance Insights

Follow the [Twillory performance workflow](.agents/skills/twillory-ad-account-performance-insights/SKILL.md). Pull from Hey Parker or Meta. Use 14 days for scaling, remarketing and retention, and 5–7 days for Creative Testing ACQ. Use ROAS with viable spend and purchase volume to judge results. Show the actual ad name and thumbnail, and identify the source creative and attribution scope. Base product allocation on acquisition spend and strategic opportunity. Add customer-profit or verified customer/order insights when available and identify their source.

## 3. Weekly testing plan

- Reconcile the current men’s and women’s plans and existing briefs, Monday tasks and launched ads before commissioning work. Distinguish net-new concepts, iterations and carry-forward decisions. Fresh evidence can change the priority of an existing concept without requiring another brief.
- Allocate test slots by SKU and actual acquisition spend, with reasons for strategic exceptions. Review product, persona and awareness-stage coverage across the slate. Mark gaps and provisional personas. Use the five awareness stages specified in [the plan-card contract](PROCESS.md#4-weekly-testing-plan-men-and-women).
- Give each concept a stable ID, plan revision, exact product, audience, awareness stage, fresh scan evidence IDs, performance rationale, hypothesis, source ad for iterations, what to hold and change, owner route, format and decision criteria. Record three distinct hook treatments and the expected deliverables. Keep proposals provisional when the evidence required for briefing is missing.

## 4. Foreplay concepting

- Inspect actual reference media in Foreplay. Capture the canonical Foreplay URL, the opening, hook, format, pacing, story, proof and CTA. Record the extent of the review and the evidence for any performance claim.
- Explain how each reference execution applies to the Twillory product and customer objection. Consider creator face-to-camera, podcast, mockumentary, green screen, drama and longer narrative when the idea fits. Include statics and GIFs where they can demonstrate the idea.
- Add the visual adaptation and source links to the plan card. Update its revision when the concept changes. Brief from the current selected revision and avoid reopening rejected duplicates. See [concepting requirements](PROCESS.md#5-concepting-and-visual-formatting).

## 5. Briefing and scripting

- Load Growthub Sales Psychology and Growthub Production Brief with the references linked in [PROCESS.md](PROCESS.md#1-reuse-existing-skills). Use Sales Psychology for the hook, proof, objection handling and CTA. Use Production Brief for the execution, shot or scene structure, storyboard and QA. Use Twillory branding and remove Growthub template branding and website footers.
- Verify exact SKU, offer, destination, claim and source assets. Align copy, script, shot list and storyboard after any revision. A carousel brief should show the order, visual and exact copy for each card when a storyboard is requested.
- Write separate sourcing, editing and design directions. Where an approved creator brief calls for natural delivery, supply benefit or feature talking points, must-capture shots and reference examples, with room for the creator’s own words. Use exact script and on-screen copy where the selected execution requires them.
- Default to H1/H2/H3 with the shared body or control specified. Request three complete video edits, three distinct static masters or three GIF variants as applicable. Cover all required hook footage in the creator brief. Follow the [hook and variant contract](PROCESS.md#6a-minimum-three-hooks-per-concept) for full requirements.
- Use Google Docs with the plan card on tab 2. Verify the intended Commenter link access, source references and readiness before sending work to production.

## 6. Production routing and QA

- Check the live Creative Production board for an existing matching concept and stage. Follow the [role matrix and read-back procedure](PROCESS.md#8-monday-prepare-write-verify-recover) for each handoff. Assign Juan to sourcing, Andrea or Richard to editing, Paola or Mac to design, and Brandon as Creative Strategist. Balance new assignments across the appropriate pool and preserve explicit assignments.
- Verify Juan’s sourcing task in the UGC Manager view with the correct status and source. Internal tasks use the approved source and reach Ready for Editing once the brief and required assets or footage are ready. Create separate tasks when video and static versions need different owners. Verify the actual product, division, category, awareness, asset type, owner, stage and brief link. Leave Priority alone.
- Track four men’s and two women’s creator briefs to Juan per week and the existing one-to-two-item expectation for Zach. Keep Zach’s exact handoff details unresolved until confirmed. Count completed handoffs by concept and finished outputs by variant.
- Check each final export against the brief for product, claim, offer, footage, copy, dimensions, variant and working destination. Link the final files and record any missing variants or approval. If a task write fails, recover the same item, verify persisted fields and report the exact remaining gap.

## 7. Launch and learning

- Use the approved assets and current account routing for authorized launch work. Confirm the exact product, offer, destination, variant and intended lane with the launch owner. Creative Testing ACQ uses product-named ABO ad sets for tests. Use the performance review to recommend mature winners for product scaling.
- Record the concept and variant ID, actual ad name, campaign and ad set, launch date, and source record when verified. Distinguish approved, launched and delivering. Record an unknown launch mapping as unresolved and verify it before attributing an outcome to the variant.
- At the next performance review, record dates, evidence and the decision to scale, iterate, keep collecting delivery or stop. Feed that learning into the next plan and provide only verified milestones in team updates. See [status and reporting](PROCESS.md#9-status-receipts-and-team-reporting).

## Keeping the workflow current

Keep reusable methods in the canonical Growthub skills and Twillory-specific rules in this repository. Keep live customer evidence, commercial figures, and working assets in their approved locations. When a standing rule changes, update the relevant section in both repositories and verify the two copies before reporting the change.
