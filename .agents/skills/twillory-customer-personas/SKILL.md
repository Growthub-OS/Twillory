# Twillory customer personas

Build each persona around a purchase decision. Identify what started the search, what the shopper wants, what might stop the purchase, and what proof would help. The same person can enter several buying situations.

## 1. Scope the evidence

- Read [WORKFLOW.md](../../../WORKFLOW.md), [PROCESS.md](../../../PROCESS.md), the latest persona map and Conversation Scan, and the current testing plan in their authorized locations. Record line, SKU family, geography when relevant, dates, and source access. Keep Men’s and Women’s separately identifiable.
- Reuse the canonical Conversation Scan linked in the workflow. Use its normal 7–10-day window for a weekly refresh. For a baseline, use a declared lookback suited to the evidence, such as roughly 90 days of relevant category or ad history. Give launch-era data its actual shorter dates.
- Include exact products and relevant formats, with enough exposure to distinguish a coverage gap from a thin audit. Note which campaigns, channels, and source types were unavailable.

## 2. Research discovery and purchase behavior

| Source | Inspect | Record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First-party shopper language | Twillory ad comments, reviews, post-purchase responses, support or return reasons when authorized | Short quote or labeled paraphrase, evidence ID, source, date, exact SKU or context, and objection. Attribute the remark to a shopper only when supported. |
| Public social | Relevant Reddit threads, visible TikTok or YouTube titles and captions, and accessible Instagram, Facebook or video comments | Observed shopper wording separately from creator hooks, captions and hashtags. Record video and comment access separately. Open source material where possible. Keep snippet-only material as a research lead. |
| Google | Run explicit product, problem, fit, and occasion queries. Inspect competitor products, review pages, editorial guides and visible result formats. Use Search Console or Trends if available for the relevant scope. | Log the exact query, date, locale when known, and what the results answer. Label suggested searches as candidates. Report volume, ranking, or momentum only with supporting measurements. |
| AI discovery | Review public examples or demos of shoppers asking AI for outfit or product advice. Identify constraints such as occasion, wardrobe, footwear and fit. | Label public examples, commercial demos, and proposed prompts distinctly. Treat an AI visibility test as a test on a named surface and date. Private prompts, individualized feeds, and complete search journeys require direct evidence. |
| Shopify | Compare first-time and returning sales and orders by SKU family. Use deduplicated order IDs for basket counts and co-purchase analysis. Examine repeat, exchanges and returns if the data supports it. | Exact product and variant grouping, period, sample and denominator. Distinguish new to Twillory from new to a line when the available data permits. |
| Paid creative | Use [Ad Account Performance Insights](../twillory-ad-account-performance-insights/SKILL.md) to pull Hey Parker or Meta delivery. Inspect the creative, opening, proof, landing page and buyer-facing message. | Ad name, SKU, lane, primary persona/message, awareness stage, spend, purchases, ROAS, date range and attribution. Report reviewed groups and spend coverage. Flag thin delivery and duplicate variants. |

Customer statements establish the reported motive or objection. Transactions establish what sold. Ads establish which messages received delivery and how the creative performed. Treat links between those sources as hypotheses until the data joins them.

## 3. Apply the right approach by line

- **Men’s.** Audit established acquisition messages against Shopify order history, recurring objections, and leadership review. For a full map, organize the strongest evidence into five currently addressed situations and five under-addressed situations or needs when supported. Show actual delivered examples for the former and the scope of the audit for the latter. Keep leadership-led hypotheses visible with their own evidence status. A survey-linked order, tracked experiment, or other defensible attribution is required before assigning new-customer sales share, CPA or LTV to a persona. Storewide or acquisition-month cohort LTV belongs to its cohort.
- **Women’s.** Build working profiles from launch orders, observed Twillory comments, the wider category scan and matching creatives. Rank first-party signals above category examples. Label thinly evidenced use cases as exploratory and note if the buyer differs from the wearer, as in gifting. Use stated purchase reasons, size and fit feedback, returns, exchanges and repeat orders to update confidence as the line matures.
- Treat a job, income, age, family situation or routine as a casting hypothesis unless directly supported. Avoid estimated segment sizes or demographic certainty based on a few comments, ad delivery, or product purchases.

## 4. Deliver the map and testing handoff

- Give each persona a buying trigger, desired outcome, purchase objection, candidate SKU, required proof, awareness stage for the proposed message, existing coverage, and a testable gap. Include source IDs, dates, confidence and what evidence would change the assessment.
- Map existing creatives to personas and link their evidence. Label what’s addressed, under-addressed, or unverified. If the task asks for the production queue, read Monday and distinguish the board’s actual awareness field from any inferred persona. Keep queued, launched, and measured work distinct.
- Translate the priority gaps into testing cards with SKU, persona, awareness stage, hook, proof, what to hold constant, and decision criteria. Use Foreplay for execution references. Where feasible, match SKU, offer, stock, placement and destination, and log landing page changes separately. Evaluate purchases and ROAS with viable spend. Use engagement, product-page behavior and fit feedback to diagnose what happened.
- Record the next validation needed. Capture why the shopper bought, who it was for, what nearly stopped them, and what they actually wore the item for. Join responses to orders only where authorized. Reclassify hypotheses as new evidence arrives.

## Repository boundary

Commit reusable methodology and templates only. Keep live account data, private artifact links, raw customer records, survey responses, and credentials in their authorized systems. Use the persona map to inform a proposal or testing plan and follow the repository’s production workflow for any handoff the user requests.
